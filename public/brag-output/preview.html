Tumaini means hope in Swahili. It is a quiet, free, and anonymous peer-support sanctuary built for young people in Uganda who need somewhere to rest and speak without being seen, judged, or asked to explain themselves.

What Tumaini Is About

A Safe Space to Speak Freely

A dedicated, zero-barrier emotional sanctuary for young Ugandans carrying tuition deadlines, family friction, grief, heartbreak, or late-night anxiety. No accounts, no phone numbers, and no real names required.

How It Helps You

Real Human Support in 3 Ways

  • 1. Confidential 1-on-1 Chat: Connect with an on-duty volunteer counselor who listens with care and zero judgment.
  • 2. Curated Group Circles: Join counselor-guided peer circles with others walking through the same burdens.
  • 3. The Confession Hearth: Release what you cannot say out loud and read anonymous words from peers across Uganda.
