About Tumaini · Our Story

Tumaini means hope in Swahili. It is a free, anonymous peer-support platform built for young people in Uganda who need somewhere to put their weight down, even for a few minutes, without being seen, judged, or asked to explain themselves.

The Problem

Mental health stigma in Uganda keeps many young people silent. Conversations about depression, anxiety, and suicidal thoughts are still often avoided or misunderstood. Existing helplines are lifelines, but they run on limited hours, require a phone call, and ask people to speak in a way that can feel exposing. For many, especially at night or mid-crisis, that gap is exactly when they go quiet.

What We Do Differently

Tumaini is not a replacement for professional care; it is the first, low-friction step toward it. Anyone can enter under an anonymous handle and talk to others carrying something similar, any hour of the day. Messages are automatically watched for crisis language, and anyone showing signs of real danger is immediately and gently pointed to Mental Health Uganda's real, staffed helpline. We complement the professional system; we do not compete with it.

Our Core Values
  • Anonymity as safety: No accounts, no real names, no data trail, so stigma cannot follow you home.
  • Peer support, honestly labeled: We never pretend to be professional counseling, and we route serious cases out to people qualified to help.
  • Built here, for here: Designed around the realities of stigma, access, and cost in Uganda, not imported wholesale from a Western wellness app.

Privacy Charter & Safe Sanctuary Terms

Tumaini is designed from the ground up as a zero-barrier safe sanctuary. We adhere to the core principles of the Uganda Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019, safeguarding your fundamental right to privacy, dignity, and confidential support.

1. Zero Personal Identifiers (No Accounts)

You are never asked for your real name, phone number, national ID (NIN), email address, or social accounts. Sessions exist solely under ephemeral nature pseudonyms or anonymous handles you choose.

2. Client-Side End-to-End Encryption (E2EE)

Every crisis message exchanged between you and on-duty counselors is encrypted directly inside your device's browser using AES-GCM (256-bit encryption keys via Web Crypto). Neither intermediate network carriers, cloud relays, nor unauthorized observers can read your conversation in transit.

3. Ephemeral Storage and Automatic Purging

Tumaini does not maintain permanent databases of user crisis conversations. Active sessions are stored only in volatile browser storage and automatically purged once resolved or after 24 hours. You can clear your session at any second using the "Quick Exit" (Esc) or "End Session" buttons.

4. Vetted Clinical Access Control

The counselor intake console is strictly restricted to vetted peer counselors and supervised clinical volunteers who authenticate with institutional supervisor keys. Unverified public access to crisis queues is barred.

5. Clinical Safety Boundaries and Duty of Care

Tumaini is an anonymous peer-support sanctuary and triage stepping stone, not a substitute for inpatient clinical psychiatry. If immediate life-threatening harm is detected, counselors will provide certified Ugandan toll-free crisis lines (e.g. Butabika Referral Hospital 0800 211 306 or Mental Health Uganda 0800 21 21 21) to connect you with emergency medical response.
