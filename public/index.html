About Tumaini · Our Story

Tumaini means hope in Swahili. It is a free, anonymous peer-support platform built for young people in Uganda who need somewhere to put their weight down, even for a few minutes, without being seen, judged, or asked to explain themselves.

The Problem

Mental health stigma in Uganda keeps many young people silent. Conversations about depression, anxiety, and suicidal thoughts are still often avoided or misunderstood. Existing helplines are lifelines, but they run on limited hours, require a phone call, and ask people to speak in a way that can feel exposing. For many, especially at night or mid-crisis, that gap is exactly when they go quiet.

What We Do Differently

Tumaini is not a replacement for professional care; it is the first, low-friction step toward it. Anyone can enter under an anonymous handle and talk to others carrying something similar, any hour of the day. Messages are automatically watched for crisis language, and anyone showing signs of real danger is immediately and gently pointed to Mental Health Uganda's real, staffed helpline. We complement the professional system; we do not compete with it.

Our Core Values
  • Anonymity as safety: No accounts, no real names, no data trail, so stigma cannot follow you home.
  • Peer support, honestly labeled: We never pretend to be professional counseling, and we route serious cases out to people qualified to help.
  • Built here, for here: Designed around the realities of stigma, access, and cost in Uganda, not imported wholesale from a Western wellness app.

Certified Uganda Crisis Helplines

If you or someone you know is in acute danger or distress, certified professional help in Uganda is free and confidential:

Butabika National Referral Mental Hospital 0800 211 306 National Toll-Free line for clinical mental health intervention
Mental Health Uganda (MHU) 0800 21 21 21 24/7 National Toll-Free counseling line, specialized for youth
StrongMinds Uganda 0800 200 600 Toll-Free psychosocial support line (Mon - Fri, 9 AM - 5 PM)
Sauti 116 (Child & GBV Helpline) 116 24/7 Toll-Free protection line for youth and domestic safety
Police & Medical Emergency Dispatch 999 / 112 Immediate emergency intervention

All lines listed above are verified Uganda numbers and free of charge from any local mobile network.
