              Important Notice: Peer Support & Crisis Stepping Stone
            
            

              Tumaini is a non-clinical anonymous peer support platform and psychological first-aid sanctuary. We are not a medical hospital, an inpatient psychiatric clinic, or an armed emergency dispatch unit.
            

          


          

            1. Scope of Practice & Non-Medical Status
            

              Our trained peer counselors and supervised volunteers provide active listening, emotional de-escalation, safety planning, and referral navigation. We do NOT diagnose psychiatric disorders, prescribe pharmaceuticals, or order involuntary psychiatric holds.
            

          


          

            2. Immediate Life-Threatening Emergencies (Uganda Hotlines)
            

              If you or someone around you is in immediate physical danger, experiencing an active suicide attempt, acute poisoning, severe domestic violence, or physical trauma, please reach out to certified 24/7 national hotlines immediately:

            3. Duty of Care & Escalation Boundaries
            

              Because Tumaini operates on strict zero-identifier privacy (no GPS geolocation, no IP logging, no phone numbers), our counselors cannot unilaterally send emergency units to your physical address. During critical incidents, counselors prioritize de-escalation and direct connection with regional responders.
            

          


          

            4. Voluntary Participation & Quick Exit
            

              Your interaction with Tumaini is entirely voluntary. You may pause or end any conversation at any time. Tapping "Quick Exit" (or pressing the Esc key) instantly clears your active screen, purges browser keys, and navigates to an educational search engine.
            

          

        


        
        

          

            
              Compliant with the Uganda Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019
            
            

              Tumaini adheres strictly to the statutory principles of data minimization, lawful processing, storage limitation, and cryptographic security under Ugandan law.
            

          


          

            1. Zero Personal Identifiers (No Mandatory Accounts)
            

              You will never be asked for your real name, National Identification Number (NIN), phone number, physical address, school/workplace affiliation, or social accounts. Interactions operate under ephemeral nature pseudonyms (e.g. Cedar Hill, River Breeze) or anonymous handles.
            

          


          

            2. Client-Side End-to-End Encryption (E2EE)
            

              Every intake ticket message is encrypted directly inside your device browser using AES-GCM (256-bit keys via the standard Web Crypto API). Neither intermediate network carriers, cloud relays, nor unauthorized database observers can inspect your messages in plaintext.
            

          


          

            3. Ephemeral Storage & Automated Purging
            

              We do not maintain permanent archival databases of your crisis consultations. Active sessions reside solely in temporary browser memory and operational database queues. Closed cases are systematically scrubbed and purged within 24 hours of session conclusion.
            

          


          

            4. Zero Commercialization & Zero Advertising
            

              Tumaini does not host commercial advertisements, affiliate trackers, or third-party marketing pixels. We will never sell, lease, or monetize your emotional reflections, confessions, or platform interactions.
            

          


          

            5. Vetted Counselor Access & Operational Auditability
            

              Counselor console access is restricted to verified clinical volunteers provisioned with cryptographic supervisor authorizations. All staff actions undergo internal audit verification to ensure ethical conduct and prevent unauthorized case tampering.
            

          


          

            6. Governance & Compliance Contact
            

              Tumaini Care Initiative operates in Kampala, Uganda. For data protection compliance inquiries or legal notices, contact our compliance desk at compliance@tumaini.care.
