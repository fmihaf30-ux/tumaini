# Brag Plan: Tumaini (5 Exact Production Showcases)

## What is this app?
Tumaini is a quiet, free, 100% anonymous peer-support sanctuary and clinical crisis triage platform built for young people in Uganda to put down heavy burdens without fear, stigma, or surveillance.

## The angle
Traditional mental health helplines ask vulnerable youth to make an exposing phone call during limited daytime hours. Tumaini gives them an exact, low-friction, anonymous bridge: choose a calm pseudonym, speak to an on-duty counselor in private 1-on-1 chat, or lay down unspoken burdens in a moderated community confession hearth.

## Video Showcase Architecture (1 Master + 4 Section Showcases)
Rather than a synthetic mockup, the studio delivers the exact, production-grade interface from the website across 5 dedicated showcases:

1. **Full Platform Tour (Master Showcase · 50s)**:
   - Complete panoramic walkthrough of Tumaini in action.
   - Real website top header, reassuring sanctuary hero, and the two platform guide cards ("What Tumaini Is About" and "How It Helps You").
   - Interactive intake triage: Handle selection, category picking (Campus & Tuition Stress), and emergency severity tiering (Tier 2 Urgent).
   - Live 1-on-1 counselor session with on-duty counselor Dr. Sarah Kigozi.
   - The Confession Room (Community Hearth) with live "Hold Space" empathy interaction.
   - Resolves on the official Tumaini brand seal.

2. **Showcase 1 (Section A1 · 25s) - The Sanctuary & Intake Triage**:
   - Focus: The public front door and intake configuration.
   - Visuals: Real header, trust pill badge, platform guide cards, anonymous handle input, 6 life stress categories, and 4 urgency tiers.
   - Action: Seeker configures intake and launches confidential chat.

3. **Showcase 2 (Section A2 · 25s) - The Confession Hearth**:
   - Focus: The communal anonymous sanctuary.
   - Visuals: Confession Room hero, safety moderation notice, submission composer, and real community cards.
   - Action: User hovers and clicks "Hold Space", watching the empathy counter increment (42 -> 43).

4. **Showcase 3 (Section B1 · 25s) - Confidential 1-on-1 Counselor Sanctuary**:
   - Focus: Live clinical chat and empathetic de-escalation.
   - Visuals: Active counselor presence (Dr. Sarah Kigozi), pulsing session dot, trauma-informed message exchange, and confidential dock.

5. **Showcase 4 (Section B2 · 25s) - Staff Clinical Command & Moderation**:
   - Focus: The internal operator terminal (`/staff`).
   - Visuals: Duty strip with live shift timer and Clock Out control, emergency triage queue sorted strictly by urgency tier (Tier 1 to Tier 4), clinical tools and de-escalation snippets, and confession moderation desk.
