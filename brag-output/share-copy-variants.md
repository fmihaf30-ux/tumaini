## 2. LinkedIn / Founder Post (Community Impact)
In Uganda, conversations around mental health, depression, and academic pressure are still too often met with silence. Traditional helplines do crucial work, but an exposing phone call during working hours can feel terrifying to a student in crisis at 2 AM.

We built Tumaini ("hope" in Swahili) as a low-friction, trauma-informed bridge.

Key Principles:
- 100% Anonymous: Shuffle a peaceful wildlife handle (e.g. Steady Kob, Quiet Crane). No accounts, no data trail.
- Confidential 1-on-1 Sanctuary: Connect directly with an on-duty volunteer counselor for gentle, grounded triage.
- The Confession Hearth: A quiet community space to lay down unspoken grief and receive quiet support ("Hold Space") from peers.
- Built for Uganda: Integrated with verified toll-free 0800 emergency helplines (Mental Health Uganda, Butabika, StrongMinds).

It does not replace professional psychiatry; it is the first, gentle step toward healing.
